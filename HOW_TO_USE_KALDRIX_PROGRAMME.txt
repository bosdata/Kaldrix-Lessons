KALDRIX DATA ANALYSIS MASTERY PROGRAMME

LEARNER
1. Open Kaldrix_Data_Analysis_Mastery_Learner.html.
2. Enter your official name.
3. Study the lesson and PDF.
4. Download the day-specific exercise dataset and import it into Google Sheets where Google Sheets is the required tool.
5. Complete the work, share the Google Sheet and paste its URL in the app.
6. Export the course record and send it to the trainer.

TRAINER
1. Open Kaldrix_Data_Analysis_Mastery_Trainer.html.
2. Import the learner course record.
3. Enter Trainer Mode, review the submission and approve or return it.
4. Export the reviewed record back to the learner.

AI LEARNING LAB
The AI Lab builds a lesson-aware coaching prompt. Copy & Open ChatGPT copies it using a clipboard fallback and opens ChatGPT. The learner pastes the prompt into ChatGPT and continues interactively. No API key is embedded in the HTML.

CERTIFICATES
A level certificate unlocks only after all 10 lessons in the level are trainer-approved and marked Done by the learner.
